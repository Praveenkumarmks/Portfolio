Data Science / ML

Context-Aware Recommendation Engine

Reads implicit behavioral signals — time spent, click frequency, scroll depth, interaction intensity — to understand what users actually want, without asking them directly. Signals are normalized into a user-item interaction matrix, and cosine similarity finds users with matching behavior patterns to generate personalized Top-N recommendations.
